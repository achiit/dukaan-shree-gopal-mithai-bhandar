Gopal Prasad Gupta

Second generation, Shree Gopal Mithai Bhandar

My father started with one kadhai and a borrowed table.

In 1987 my father, Shri Ram Gopal Gupta, began selling jalebi outside this same spot on Station Road. I was nine and my job was to wash the trays before school. I still remember the smell of ghee on my school shirt.

Today I stand at the same counter. Some of you buying laddus for your daughter's wedding are the children who once bought two-rupee jalebis from my father. That is the kind of customer we keep.

We do not chase trends. We make what your grandmother would recognise, with the same ghee and the same patience.

  • Only pure desi ghee. Bill and supplier shown if you ask.
  • Everything made fresh that morning, never reheated.
  • You may taste before you buy, always.
  • Sugar-light options for elders on request.
  • Same recipes since 1987.
